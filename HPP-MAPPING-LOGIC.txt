HPP MAPPING LOGIC
==================

Source: /home/yogaimawan/Dokumentasi/shopee_profit_estimation/README.md
Validated: 2026-08-06

---

PRIORITY ORDER (4 steps):

STEP 1: Get SKU from Order.all
  Priority 1: Nomor Referensi SKU (check first)
  Priority 2: SKU Induk (fallback if empty)

STEP 2: Match to master.xlsx
  Priority 1: SKU1 (check first)
  Priority 2: SKU2 (fallback if not found)

STEP 3: Get Result
  - Harga = HPP (already includes packaging cost)
  - IDPRODUK = Universal product identifier

---

SQL LOGIC:

COALESCE(orders.nomor_referensi_sku, orders.sku_induk) 
  = master.sku1 
  OR 
COALESCE(orders.nomor_referensi_sku, orders.sku_induk) 
  = master.sku2

---

EXAMPLE (No. Pesanan: 2607072CRRDA37):

Order.all:
  Nomor Referensi SKU: "M-TAC Pendek" ✓
  SKU Induk: (kosong)
  → Use: "M-TAC Pendek"

master.xlsx:
  SKU1: "M-TAC Pendek" ← MATCH!
  SKU2: "MTAC PENDEK"
  Harga: 52,500
  IDPRODUK: "M-TAC Pendek"

Result:
  HPP = Rp52,500 ✓

Profit Calculation:
  Net Payout (from Income): Rp66,193
  HPP (from master): Rp52,500
  ───────────────────────────────────
  Profit = Rp13,693 ✓

---

NOTES:
- HPP di master.xlsx sudah FINAL (includes packaging)
- IDPRODUK = universal identifier across all stores
- SKU1/SKU2 = variants/aliases for flexible matching
- Case-insensitive matching recommended
- Multiple rows in master can have same SKU1 (same price)
